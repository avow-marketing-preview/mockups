05FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I request just a logo?

Yes. Choose Logo, Website, or Both in the request form.

Do I need an existing website?

No. The current website field is optional.

Is the mockup free?

Yes. The mockup request is for a free design concept.

Is hosting included?

Avow offers free hosting for new websites. Hosting is separate from the free-mockup design request.

Will the new site work on phones?

Always. Every site is designed for phones first: readable text, easy-to-tap buttons, a simple menu, and no sideways scrolling, then scaled up for tablets and desktops.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so Avow quotes each project after understanding what you need. The mockup is free either way.
