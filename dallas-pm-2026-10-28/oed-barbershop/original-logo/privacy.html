Privacy policy

This page explains how Old East Dallas Barbershop uses the details you send through this website’s contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form: your name, how to reach you and your message. The form doesn’t store anything on this website. Submitting opens your own email app, addressed to oedbarbershop@gmail.com.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your message. We don’t sell or share them.

Visit counts

This preview counts page visits with GoatCounter, which doesn’t use cookies or collect personal details.

Questions

Ask us in person or through the contact form and we’ll help.
