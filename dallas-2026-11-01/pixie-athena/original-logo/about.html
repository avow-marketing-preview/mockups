Pixie Athena’s story

Soulful, determined, refined.

Photos by @theonlyalew (opens in a new tab) & @chrissimmonsphotography (opens in a new tab)

Athena is a soulful, determined, refined artist with a well-trained ear and a DIY attitude.

Fuelled by an unrelenting desire to create soundscapes and stories, Pixie Athena has spent the last five years writing, performing, producing, engineering, releasing and marketing her work. Since the age of 10 she has sung in vocal organizations, including the Children’s Chorus of Greater Dallas.

In high school she joined the Dallas Youth Poets slam poetry team, which competed globally, including at Brave New Voices. Its leader, slam poet and author Joaquin Zihuatanejo, inspired her to combine poems and music into songwriting. By 17 she was writing ballads on her Korg keyboard.

She went on to study songwriting and production/engineering at the University of North Texas, alongside years of self-taught piano and DAW practice. Her songs stream on all major platforms.

She plays shows across the DFW metroplex on her self-produced tracks, sometimes as a stripped acoustic set with multi-instrumentalist Brennan Davis, or solo. Collaborating with artists such as Royall has been especially well received.

Entranced by dance and disco, her latest work is upbeat and energetic. Her single “Invincible” was featured on 91.7 FM KXT, a division of KERA.
