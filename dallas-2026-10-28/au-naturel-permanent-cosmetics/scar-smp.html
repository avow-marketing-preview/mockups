Scar camouflage & SMP

Corrective, medical tattooing

Specialized tattooing to improve the look of hypopigmented (white) scars and scalp density concerns, using natural pigment tones that blend with skin.

Scar camouflage

Surgical and injury scars can be treated when they are hypopigmented, meaning they lack melanin. Pigment is matched to your skin tone to create the blended illusion of a smoother, more even surface.

As a guideline, wait at least 9–12 months after surgery, and talk with your doctor first. We only treat hypopigmented scars, such as those around the hairline from cosmetic facelifts, and other minor surgical or accident scars.

Scalp follicle density (SMP)

Pigment matched to hair follicle color helps restore the look of density. Cost depends on the size of the area and needs a consultation, with photos if virtual.

  • Scar camouflage / revision: from $275
  • Scalp follicle density SMP: from $375
