Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the details you share through the contact form on this website.

What the form does

The form does not send or store anything on this website. When you press the send button, your own email app opens with a pre-filled message addressed to bookings@losgranreyes.com. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

What we receive

We receive only what you choose to include in that email, such as your name, email address, phone number and the details of your question.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you about what you asked. We do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Analytics

This site may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free visit counting to see which pages are useful. It does not identify you.

Questions or removal

To ask what we hold or to have your details deleted, email bookings@losgranreyes.com.
