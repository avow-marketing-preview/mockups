Our story

From a 10×10 suite to a full beauty team

B-Lashed started in 2014 as B.madebybebe LLC, a one-woman business in a 10×10 suite. It has grown into a full beauty team with a storefront.

Founder Bebe Tran trained in cosmetology at TONI&GUY and with Dior Cosmetics, and holds a BA in fashion design & merchandising.
