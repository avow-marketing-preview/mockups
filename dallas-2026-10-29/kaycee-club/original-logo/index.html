Our history

The KayCee Club, Inc. since 1958

The KayCee Club is the sister organization to the Knights of Columbus, Dallas Council 799, chartered in 1903 and the second oldest Council in Texas.

  1. 1903

    Council 799 is chartered on May 9, with its first hall at 1914 Main Street.

  2. 1915

    The Council buys its first property, at 2520 Ross Avenue.

  3. 1951

    It buys “The Showboat” on East Northwest Highway, an 8-acre Dallas landmark.

  4. 1958

    The property is transferred to the KayCee Club, Inc.

  5. 1999

    The current building is blessed and dedicated on May 16.
