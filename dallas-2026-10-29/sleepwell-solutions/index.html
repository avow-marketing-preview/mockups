Sleep and breathing

Snoring is the first red flag

Sleep disordered breathing is a spectrum, from mild snoring to severe sleep apnea. Even soft snoring can cause “micro-arousals” that keep you from deep, restorative sleep.

Mild snoringSevere sleep apnea

Non-restorative sleep

  • Irritability and mood swings
  • Daytime sleepiness
  • Morning headaches
  • Depression / anxiety

Untreated apnea is linked to

  • Chronic sleepiness
  • Heartburn
  • High blood pressure
  • Heart attack and stroke

Tap into sleep

Treatments built around you

Oral appliances, a strap-free CPAP mask, and combinations of the two for complex cases.

Oral appliance

Custom TAP

We offer several custom, fully adjustable mandibular advancement designs: Nylon flexTAP, flexTAP, dreamTAP and TAP 3. Made from dental models, the TAP clicks into place and gently holds the jaw slightly forward so the airway doesn’t collapse.

  • Side-to-side jaw movement, essential for patients who grind their teeth
  • Micro-adjustments you control
  • Most patients, even with severe sleep apnea, see results the first night
Quick start

myTAP

One of the quickest and most cost-effective therapies for snoring and mild-to-moderate obstructive sleep apnea: two low-profile trays molded to your teeth, with a simple adjustment mechanism.

  • A trial device when considering oral appliance therapy
  • A temporary treatment for snoring during pregnancy
  • A “travel” device instead of taking CPAP on trips
Invented by Dr. Thornton

myTAP PAP Nasal Pillow Mask

A thin mouthguard on the upper teeth holds the nasal pillows in place for a perfect seal, so head straps are no longer needed. We’ll help fit one no matter where you bought your PAP machine.

  • Less air leakage
  • No “facial marks” from tight straps
  • Less claustrophobia, better CPAP compliance
For complex cases

TAP PAP combination & custom solutions

When neither CPAP nor an oral appliance alone is enough, the two work together, so CPAP pressures can be lower. For difficult cases, Dr. Thornton’s team builds custom solutions and works closely with respiratory specialists.

  • Perfect seal between mask and face
  • Lower air pressures from the CPAP machine
  • Mask placement with one hand, for limited mobility

Insurance & financing

Out-of-network insurance

We’re out-of-network for most plans and help you submit claims for reimbursement.

Ask your insurer whether your plan covers a mandibular advancement device for obstructive sleep apnea. The code for a custom device is E0486.

Medicare

SleepWell Solutions is not contracted with Medicare Part B. Medicare covers an oral appliance for obstructive sleep apnea every 5 years with a sleep study less than 5 years old. We can help you file.
