Preventive maintenance

Maintenance plans

Routine service keeps your system in top working condition and helps prevent deterioration over time. We recommend it twice a year: before the hot summer, and again before winter.

Option 1

Spring & fall

We come out once in the spring and again in the fall. Two cleanings and inspections per year.

  • Inspection and cleaning of your heating and cooling equipment
  • We replace your air filter each time we visit
Option 2

One spring visit

If you replace your own filter, this may be better for you. We service the A/C and the heater while we’re at your home in the spring.

  • No need to schedule us back in the fall
  • Saves you an additional $20
  • Problem in the fall? We return with no trip charge

Do

  • Use filters, and check them every 3 to 4 weeks.
  • Keep windows and doors closed, and pull shades on windows in the sun.
  • Turn on kitchen exhaust fans when cooking.
  • Do your “heat and moisture” work, like washing and mopping, in the morning or evening.
  • Keep attic ventilators open.

Don’t

  • Don’t be a “thermostat jiggler.” Set it and forget it.
  • Don’t set the thermostat too low. Most people find 76 to 78 degrees ideal.
  • Don’t block registers with furniture.
  • Don’t let shrubs, vines or fences block the outdoor unit.
  • Don’t put a lamp or TV too near your thermostat.
