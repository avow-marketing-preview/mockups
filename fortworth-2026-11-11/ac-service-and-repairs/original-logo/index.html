Symptom checker

System isn’t working? Try these first.

Is it getting power? Is the thermostat on and set to heating or cooling? If so, pick the symptom that fits best.

No heat or not enough heat

  1. Replace the batteries in your thermostat. Check that it’s on and set to the correct temperature.
  2. Check the air filters in each of your system components. Dirty filters can cause severe problems with your unit.
  3. Close any open doors or windows; warm air may be escaping through them.
  4. Check your circuit breakers or fuse box. Switch on an open breaker or replace a burned-out fuse.
  5. New or remodeled home, or recent work on your fuel or electric lines? Check whether your gas or electricity has been turned off.

No cooling or not enough cooling

  1. Replace the batteries in your thermostat. Check that it’s on and set to the correct temperature.
  2. Check the air filters in each of your system components. Dirty filters can cause severe problems with your unit.
  3. Close any open doors or windows; cool air may be escaping through them.
  4. Check your circuit breakers or fuse box. Switch on an open breaker or replace a burned-out fuse.
  5. Remove any leaves or debris restricting air flow to your outdoor unit.
Still not working?Call (817) 737-8423

No air flow

  1. Check around your outdoor unit. Leaves, hedges or walls butting up against it can freeze the system up due to a dirty coil. Give it 6 inches of clearance all around.
  2. Check the air filters in each of your system components.
  3. No air coming through your vents? Your indoor blower may not be operating. Give us a call.
Still not working?Call (817) 737-8423

Stale, stuffy air

  1. If you have a whole-home air cleaner or air exchanger, make sure it’s switched on and its filter is clean.
Still not working?Call (817) 737-8423

Too dry, or too much moisture

  1. Make sure your humidifier is switched on. Many homeowners turn it off at the end of heating season and forget to turn it back on.
  2. Make sure the humidifier’s damper or water valve is open.
  3. Check your humidifier setting and adjust the indoor relative humidity for greater comfort.
Still not working?Call (817) 737-8423

Noisy air vents

  1. A high-pitched sound often, although not always, means a lack of return air. Make sure your return and supply vents are open and not blocked by furniture.
  2. Rattling, humming, thumping or scraping could mean undersized or flimsy duct work, a clogged filter, or wear on internal components. If you hear an unusual sound, call us.
