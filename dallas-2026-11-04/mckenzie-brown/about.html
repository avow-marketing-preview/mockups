Who is this chick

Better hooks, fewer footnotes

McKenzie Brown writes songs the way some people write manifestos, only hers come with better hooks and fewer footnotes. An Oak Cliff troubadour peddling heartbreak and rebellion, she wrings soul from Americana like it owes her money.

She got her start playing for hours on a Bishop Arts street corner and has since worked her way into air-conditioned rooms across North Texas. She holds residencies at Troy’s in Arlington and Lakewood Brewing, and was a member of the inaugural Dallas Sounds busking cohort.

In her own words: “Crestfallen Oak Cliff, TX lover and fighter. I just work here, man.”
