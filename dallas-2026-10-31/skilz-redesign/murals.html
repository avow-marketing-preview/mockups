Questions

Mural FAQs

How much does a mural cost?+

Mural pricing depends on size, location, timeline, design complexity and accessibility. After talking through your project, you get a custom quote. A deposit and an approved design concept are typically required to reserve a mural date.

How long does a mural take?+

It depends on size and detail. Typical mural projects take anywhere from one day to one week.

What paint do you use?+

High-quality aerosol spray paint. It is durable, quick-drying and works indoors or outdoors, and it can last several years depending on weather exposure.

Will I see a sketch first?+

Not necessarily; contracts vary with each client’s needs. There is always an approval process before painting starts. A sketch is optional and can require an additional deposit.

Do I need permission?+

Yes. You need the building owner’s permission to paint indoors or outdoors. Check with your local county for any permit or signage rules.

Does the wall need prep?+

In most cases the artist will advise on basic prep. Some surfaces need cleaning, priming or minor repairs before painting.

Do you live-paint at events?+

Yes. Get in touch with the details.
