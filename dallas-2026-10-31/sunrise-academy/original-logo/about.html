Our story

It started at summer camp

Sunrise Academy was formed in 2020 by childhood friends Yakob Dye and Julian Sol Jordan, who met at a summer camp in Texas at 13.

Their songs are about growing up, love and the moments that stay with us. Their latest project is Don’t Miss the Field Trip, and they’re preparing live shows.

Outside the band, Yakob has a solo R&B project and Julian is a filmmaker.
