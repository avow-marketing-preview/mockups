About

Jose May, aka Skilz.

Artist Jose May, better known as “Skilz”, is a resident muralist in the Dallas area with over 20 years of experience creating bold, expressive artwork. Working primarily with spray paint, his style blends graffiti, contemporary and abstract design.

Skilz taught himself the fundamentals of drawing and painting, and later joined one of Dallas’s early graffiti crews, Infinite Crew (IC), where he became known for colorful, imaginative character work.

His murals are found throughout Dallas-Fort Worth, including Deep Ellum, Arlington and Plano. In 2017 he partnered with local artist Pkin to launch Art Loot 52, built around one idea: “Look Closer. Enjoy More.”

Under his own name, Jose May, he also makes contemporary fine art exploring abstraction, color study and layered composition.
